# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: adults living in the UK or abroad (largely Polish and Spanish speakers) who need a language, mostly English, for work and everyday life, and who carry anxiety about speaking it. They arrive curious, comparing schools, and want to understand quickly what Lingolandias is and whether it is a safe place to start. Secondary (unconfirmed priority): adults learning Spanish, Italian, Indonesian or Polish for personal growth; parents seeking Polish as a second language for children/teens (incl. GCSE Polish).

## Product Purpose

Lingolandias is an online language school with live classes. It teaches languages with attention to emotions, body and mind so learners can speak with confidence. Success: a visitor understands the offer, trusts the school, and takes a first step (level test, WhatsApp contact, or a course).

## Positioning

A human, emotionally safe school: mistakes are part of the process, nobody rolls their eyes while you search for a word. Combines native and non-native teachers (people who know what it is to be in your place), personal-growth workshops, free group classes, language challenges and "Spill the Tea" conversation meetups. Learn Spanish and Italian through English (practice two languages at once).

## Operating Context

Fully online, live classes. Contact via WhatsApp (+44 7578 146264) and /contact. Free level tests exist for English (/english-quiz) and Spanish (/spanish-quiz) via /test. Site is trilingual: Spanish, English, Polish (i18next).

## Capabilities and Constraints

React 18 + Vite + Tailwind 3 + react-router-dom + i18next. Existing routes: /, /courses, /team, /enteachers, /esteachers, /test, /spanish-quiz, /english-quiz, /contact, workshops page. Hero primary action is undecided by the owner; design should propose it.

Languages offered: English (all levels), Spanish, Italian, Polish as a second language, Indonesian.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Lingolandias, "online language school". Logo: circular seal with a white serif "L" framed by two corner brackets, on a purple/orange/teal painted gradient, ringed by the text "Lingolandias online language school". The owner explicitly rejects the current site's look (space background, stars, floating words, gradient headline). Founder photo is not required in the hero. Binding copy (requested by the founder, must survive any redesign): "Habla desde el corazón. Vive con autenticidad. Crea tu realidad." (EN: "Speak from the heart. Live authentically. Create your reality.").

## Evidence on Hand

Stated figures in current copy: 300+ students, 5+ years, 6 languages (owner treats as real). Teacher photos in src/assets. Facebook reviews section exists (Reviews.jsx). No pricing, no named corporate clients, no press: do not fabricate.

## Product Principles

1. Safety before performance: the visitor should feel they can start without being judged.
2. Clarity first: what languages, how classes happen, how to begin, within seconds.
3. Human, not corporate: real teachers and community over stock claims.
4. One obvious next step on every screen.
